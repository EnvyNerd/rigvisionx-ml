# TerraEnergy AI ESG & Sustainability

## Environmental
- Reduce energy waste by monitoring AEOS energy deviations and flagging inefficiencies.
- Cut emissions indirectly by preventing unplanned downtime and optimizing maintenance cycles.
- Support energy benchmarking across rigs and time periods to track improvements.

## Social
- Improve worker safety by detecting anomalies early and reducing high-risk failures.
- Increase operational transparency through explainable outputs and clear reporting.
- Support training and upskilling with dashboards and interpretable analytics.

## Governance
- Enforce role-based access control with admin/viewer permissions.
- Maintain audit-ready records of training jobs and dataset metadata in SQLite.
- Enable policy enforcement for demo/licensing and controlled access to protected endpoints.

## Sustainability Outcomes
- Longer asset life via predictive maintenance and RUL insights.
- Lower total cost of ownership through proactive maintenance planning.
- Data-driven decisions that balance performance, risk, and energy efficiency.

# TerraEnergy AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 10, 2026

TerraEnergy AI ("we", "us", "our") provides predictive maintenance and energy optimization software, including API, dashboard, and desktop tools.

## 1. Information We Collect

- Account data: username, encrypted password hash, role, and last login time.
- Operational data: CSV uploads, dataset paths, training job metadata, and generated reports.
- Technical data: authentication tokens and request metadata required for security and service operation.
- Local configuration data: API URLs, optional API keys/tokens, and environment settings.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To authenticate users and enforce role-based access control.
- To run training, prediction, analytics, and reporting features.
- To maintain service reliability, security, and troubleshooting.
- To enforce licensing and demo restrictions.

## 3. Storage and Retention

- Data is stored in your configured infrastructure (for example local files and SQLite).
- You control retention by deleting files, uploads, or database records.
- We retain only data needed to provide and secure software functionality.

## 4. Data Sharing

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not share your data except when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or when you configure third-party integrations.

## 5. Security

- Passwords are stored as one-way bcrypt hashes.
- API authentication uses JWT or API key mechanisms depending on deployment mode.
- You are responsible for secure deployment, credential management, backups, and access restrictions in your environment.

## 6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your account-related personal data.

Contact: legal@terraenergy-ai.com

## 7. Children's Privacy

This software is not intended for children under 13.

## 8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy and will update the Effective Date when changes are published.

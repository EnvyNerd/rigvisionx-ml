# TerraEnergy AI Story (Cinematic)

The rig wakes before dawn, sensors humming. TerraEnergy AI inhales raw time-series, cleans it, windows it, and turns it into features that the models understand. Failure risk rises like a warning flare, remaining useful life counts down in quiet numbers, and anomalies surface as ripples in the flow.

Energy tells its own story—AEOS deviations reveal waste, cost, and opportunity. The decision engine fuses risk, anomalies, and RUL into a single directive: act now, or wait, or schedule the fix that keeps the whole operation moving.

On the dashboard, the system becomes visible: live charts, training panels, and explainable insights. Operators train models on fresh CSVs, benchmark fleets, and watch predictions settle into confidence. Finance runs the numbers—ROI, forecasts, comparisons, savings—turning maintenance into strategy.

Behind the scenes, FastAPI serves every request, authenticated by JWT and bcrypt, backed by SQLite, configured by YAML, powered by Python, NumPy, pandas, and scikit-learn. SHAP opens the black box. Reports become PDFs. The demo clock can start, the license can unlock, and Docker can launch the stack in minutes.

TerraEnergy AI doesn’t just predict. It orchestrates uptime, efficiency, and decisions—one dataset, one dashboard, one deployment at a time.

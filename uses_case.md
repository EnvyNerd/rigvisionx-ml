# TerraEnergy AI Use Cases

- Predictive maintenance for industrial equipment using failure risk and RUL
- Early anomaly detection in sensor streams to prevent unplanned downtime
- Energy efficiency monitoring with AEOS deviation analysis
- Risk fusion and decision support for maintenance prioritization
- Cost and ROI analysis for maintenance and operations (fintech reports)
- Fleet-level monitoring across multiple rigs with synthetic or real data
- Rapid demo/POC deployment using demo mode and one-click launch
- Training and benchmarking models on time-series CSV datasets
- Operational dashboards for live monitoring and insights
